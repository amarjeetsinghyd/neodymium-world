The luxury market's pivot to AI entrepreneurs represents a fundamental wealth redistribution pattern.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I forecast that within 24 months, non-US AI models will capture significant market share in regions outside US influence. India's dual-track approach—embracing global AI partnerships while developing indigenous capabilities—positions it optimally in this fragmented landscape. Luxury brands that successfully capture AI wealth will establish lasting market advantages in the emerging ultra-wealthy segment.</p>
