<h2>Introduction</h2><p>The United Nations has issued a stark warning about AI-powered misinformation inciting harm to refugees worldwide. This digital threat represents a new frontier in information warfare that directly impacts regional stability and India's security interests. The UNHCR's findings at the AI for Good Global Summit reveal how technological advancement has created new vulnerabilities in humanitarian contexts.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>I analyze this situation through the lens of South Asian geopolitics, where India faces complex refugee challenges from neighboring countries. The spread of misinformation targeting refugees creates fertile ground for extremist narratives that could destabilize border regions. India's strategic autonomy requires developing indigenous capabilities to counter digital threats while maintaining humanitarian commitments.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I project that AI-powered disinformation campaigns will increasingly target refugee populations in South Asia, with India bearing disproportionate impact due to its regional position. The weaponization of information against vulnerable populations represents a direct threat to India's internal security and social cohesion. Without decisive action, we can expect a dangerous convergence of refugee crises and digital manipulation tactics.</p><h2>India's Strategic Imperatives</h2><p>India must develop a comprehensive framework to counter AI-powered misinformation while strengthening its Atmanirbhar Bharat capabilities in digital defense. The nation should establish regional partnerships to share intelligence on disinformation campaigns targeting refugee populations. I recommend India lead a coalition of like-minded nations to develop technological solutions for humanitarian information integrity.</p>
