India's current defense robotics capabilities cannot match China's integrated civilian-military development model.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I project China will deploy humanoid robots in border surveillance and logistics within 3-5 years, creating asymmetric advantages over Indian forces. India must accelerate its Atmanirbhar Bharat initiatives in defense robotics or face strategic technological inferiority.</p>
