The enhanced financial delegation should enable DRDO to fast-track critical projects like hypersonic technology, quantum communications, and advanced AI-based defense systems.</p><h2>Geopolitical Impact</h2><p>This reform strengthens India's position in the Indo-Pacific security architecture.
