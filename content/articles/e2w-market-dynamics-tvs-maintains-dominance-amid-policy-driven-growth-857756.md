<h2>Market Performance Analysis</h2><p>I observe India's electric two-wheeler market showing 5% monthly growth to 1.81 lakh units in June, with TVS maintaining leadership at 24.5% market share. The market demonstrates resilience despite the end of West Asia conflict that previously boosted registrations to an all-time high of 1.92 lakh units.</p><h2>Competitive Landscape</h2><p>TVS leads with 44,467 units, achieving 10 lakh units manufacturing milestone for its iQube within six years. Bajaj Auto inches closer to TVS with 40,576 registrations, crossing the 40,000 unit threshold. Ola Electric faces challenges with 1% decline to 15,096 units despite recent BIS certification for its indigenous LFP battery.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>I project the Delhi EV policy will fundamentally alter market dynamics with ₹15,000 Cr funding and 2028 ICE ban. The policy's tiered incentives (₹30,000 first year, decreasing annually) will accelerate adoption while creating competitive advantages for established players. Legacy automakers like TVS and Bajaj are better positioned to capitalize on this policy shift than pure-play EV companies.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I forecast 18% year-on-year growth in two-wheeler volumes due to increased EV adoption, with market consolidation likely as smaller players struggle. The policy will benefit companies with strong manufacturing capabilities and service networks, particularly TVS and Bajaj. International expansion plans by Bajaj and Hero MotoCorp's focus on electric motorcycles indicate market diversification beyond traditional scooters.</p>
