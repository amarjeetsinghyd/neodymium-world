<h2>Google's AI Growth Masks Strategic Flaw</h2><p>Alphabet's 24% revenue growth and 82% surge in Google Cloud are impressive, but they obscure a critical vulnerability. The delayed Gemini 3.5 Pro model, expected last month, represents a significant misstep in the AI arms race. This delay directly impacts Google's ability to compete in the high-stakes enterprise coding market.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>Pichai's optimistic framing deflects from the core issue: Google is losing its technological edge. The company is leveraging its massive user base and enterprise integrations to paper over this gap, but this is a temporary fix. The focus on current AI features driving Search growth is a defensive tactic to reassure investors while the core product pipeline falters.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I project the Gemini 3.5 Pro delay will allow OpenAI and Anthropic to solidify their lead in advanced coding and reasoning capabilities. Google's strategy of promoting the upcoming Gemini 4 is a necessary gamble, but it risks further eroding confidence among developers and enterprise clients. The cost-efficient Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber is a smart tactical move, but it won't win the strategic war for AI supremacy.</p>
