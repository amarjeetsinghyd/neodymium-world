This shift fundamentally alters the geopolitical calculus in the region.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>I've analyzed this development through the lens of power transition theory. The EU's outreach to India reflects a broader recognition of multipolar realities. European defense industries see India as a lucrative market and technology partner, while India gains access to cutting-edge defense systems.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I project this partnership will accelerate India's defense indigenization through technology transfer agreements. The EU's strategic autonomy drive makes India an attractive partner beyond traditional Western alliances.</p><h2>Market Implications</h2><p>European defense contractors will gain unprecedented access to India's $250 billion defense market.
