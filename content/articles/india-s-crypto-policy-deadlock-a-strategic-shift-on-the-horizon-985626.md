<h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>I view this parliamentary intervention as a calculated political maneuver to manage a complex technological disruption. The committee's recommendations, while non-binding, represent the most significant official acknowledgment of the crypto sector's existence and its regulatory gaps. This is a clear signal that the government is moving from a stance of containment to one of controlled integration.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I project that the interim SRO framework will be operational within 12-18 months, serving as a testing ground for regulatory principles. This will likely lead to a formal, multi-regulator structure where SEBI oversees tokenised securities, the RBI governs stablecoins, and a new body handles crypto-native assets. The ultimate outcome will be a more mature market with clearer rules, attracting institutional capital while preserving the government's strategic oversight.</p><h2>The Regulatory Awakening</h2><p>For years, India's crypto policy has been a blunt instrument of taxation and AML compliance. The committee's report signals a sophisticated pivot towards nuanced regulation. It formally recognizes the regulatory vacuum created by excluding VDAs from the securities framework, a critical first step.</p><h2>A Fragmented Future</h2><p>The industry's push for a multi-regulator model is not just a defensive tactic; it's a strategic imperative. Treating VDAs as a single asset class is the root cause of the current ambiguity. A segmented approach based on economic function—tokenised securities, payment assets, and crypto-native assets—is the only logical path forward.</p><h2>Tokenisation's Unblocked Potential</h2><p>The committee's technology-neutral approach to the Securities Markets Code is a masterstroke. It decouples the regulatory treatment of an asset from its underlying technology. This clarification will empower market infrastructure institutions to issue tokenised bonds and funds with greater confidence, accelerating the evolution of financial market infrastructure.</p>
