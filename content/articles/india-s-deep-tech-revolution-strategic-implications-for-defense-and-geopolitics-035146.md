The transition from technology consumer to creator represents a fundamental shift in India's geopolitical positioning. This transformation directly impacts defense preparedness and regional power dynamics.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>Dr. Mashelkar's emphasis on affordable innovation aligns with India's strategic need for resource-efficient defense technologies. The 'More for Less for More' philosophy directly addresses India's unique challenges of maintaining military superiority with constrained resources. I observe this approach mirrors successful asymmetric warfare strategies employed by regional powers.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I project India's deep-tech capabilities will revolutionize its defense procurement within the next decade. The integration of AI as 'co-pilot' rather than 'autopilot' ensures human control over critical defense systems. This balanced approach positions India as a responsible technological power while maintaining strategic autonomy.</p><h2>Geopolitical Implications</h2><p>India's innovation ecosystem development will create new dependencies and partnerships across South Asia. The country's ability to produce cost-effective defense technologies reduces vulnerability to external pressure. This technological self-reliance strengthens India's negotiating position in regional security arrangements.</p>
