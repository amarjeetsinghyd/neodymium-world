<h2>India's Defense Expenditure Trajectory</h2><p>I project India's defense capital expenditure will surge to ₹2.8 trillion by FY2030, representing an aggressive 11% CAGR that fundamentally reshapes South Asian military capabilities. This spending trajectory reflects a strategic pivot from import dependency to indigenous manufacturing, aligning with the Atmanirbhar Bharat vision while creating a self-sustaining defense industrial ecosystem.</p><h2>Key Growth Drivers</h2><p>Drones and counter-drone systems will emerge as critical growth vectors, with domestic procurement leading this transformation as India seeks to address asymmetric warfare challenges. The export potential, projected at ₹500 billion by FY2029, positions India to challenge traditional defense suppliers while establishing strategic partnerships across South and Southeast Asia.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>I observe that India's defense indigenization drive represents more than mere procurement strategy—it's a geopolitical imperative that reduces vulnerability to supply chain disruptions while creating technological sovereignty. The convergence of domestic manufacturing capabilities with export ambitions creates a virtuous cycle where revenue from exports funds further R&D, accelerating India's emergence as a defense technology powerhouse.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I forecast that India's defense spending trajectory will catalyze a regional arms race, compelling neighboring countries to accelerate their own modernization programs in response. The indigenous development of critical technologies, particularly in unmanned systems and electronic warfare, will provide India with asymmetric advantages that offset conventional force disparities. By FY2030, India will likely transition from being the world's largest arms importer to a significant net exporter, fundamentally altering global defense market dynamics.</p>
