<h2>India's Missile Arsenal: Strategic Credibility Through Combat Validation and Exports</h2><p>India's missile arsenal has achieved significant global credibility through combat validation and strategic exports. The Agni, Pralay, and BrahMos systems provide layered deterrence capabilities that rival established powers. I project this technological advancement will reshape regional power dynamics and enhance India's strategic autonomy.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>I observe that India's missile development trajectory demonstrates sophisticated indigenous innovation. The Agni series extends nuclear deterrence with ranges up to 8,000 kilometers and MIRV technology. Pralay fills the tactical gap with maneuverable precision capabilities. BrahMos represents a successful Indo-Russian partnership that has evolved into a standalone export product.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I project India's missile exports will accelerate as combat validation increases buyer confidence. The layered capability creates a comprehensive deterrence architecture that appeals to multiple strategic partners. I forecast these exports will become a significant tool of Indian foreign policy, particularly in the Indo-Pacific region.</p><h2>Market Analysis</h2><p>The global missile market presents substantial opportunities for Indian manufacturers. Atmanirbhar Bharat initiatives are reducing import dependency while enhancing export competitiveness. I analyze that India's cost-effective production models will capture market share from traditional suppliers.</p>
