The ten-squadron fleet creates an integrated air defense umbrella from the Himalayas to the Arabian Sea. This capability directly counters China's PLAAF advantage along the Line of Actual Control.</p><h2>Geopolitical Ramifications</h2><p>The S-400 expansion triggers strategic realignment across the region. Pakistan's air force faces immediate capability degradation against India's enhanced defenses. China must now factor India's air defense network into all operational planning.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>I assess this acquisition as India's most significant defense investment since the 1971 war. The S-400's 400km range creates overlapping kill zones across strategic approaches. This network forces adversaries to operate at extreme standoff distances.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I forecast China will accelerate its own air defense modernization to counter India's S-400 deployment. Pakistan will seek alternative suppliers like China or Russia for advanced air defense systems. The regional arms race will intensify, with India maintaining technological superiority.</p>
