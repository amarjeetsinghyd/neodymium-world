These startups are building the digital infrastructure for Atmanirbhar Bharat by solving deep-seated financial inclusion challenges.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>ProgShakti's model is a masterclass in identifying and solving a specific market failure. This approach directly enhances female labor force participation, a critical metric for India's economic future.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I project that AI-driven credit assessment will become the standard for Indian lending within five years. Ezolv's focus on automating risk assessment and debt collection will force traditional lenders to innovate or become obsolete. The success of these platforms will create a data-rich environment, further refining AI models and creating a competitive moat for early movers.</p><h2>Market Implications</h2><p>The funding in specialized healthtech like Lissun indicates a move away from generic solutions. This trend will likely see more capital flow into niche B2B and B2C healthtech applications with clear, high-value outcomes.</p>
