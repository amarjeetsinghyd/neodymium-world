The targeting of financial operatives and propaganda heads indicates India's comprehensive approach to countering terror financing and radicalization networks.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I forecast India will continue expanding its terrorist designation list as part of its broader strategy to isolate Pakistan's terror infrastructure internationally. The designations provide diplomatic leverage in bilateral engagements while strengthening India's position in multilateral counterterrorism forums.</p>
