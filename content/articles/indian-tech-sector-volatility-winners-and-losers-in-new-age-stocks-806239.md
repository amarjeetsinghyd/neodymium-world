<h2>Market Overview</h2><p>Indian equities experienced a volatile week with new-age tech stocks showing mixed performance. While 29 of 57 new-age tech companies gained between 0.24% to over 15%, 27 fell between 0.37% to 9.11%. The cumulative market capitalization of 58 new-age tech companies stood at $134.45 Bn, down from $139.29 Bn the previous week.</p><h2>Top Performers</h2><p>MapmyIndia emerged as the biggest gainer, surging 15.38% to close at ₹1,083.50. PhysicsWallah followed with a 13.5% increase to end at ₹147.95. Nine stocks including RateGain, Shadowfax, Lenskart, Honasa Consumer, Delhivery, Kissht, Aequs, Nykaa, Ather Energy and Amagi touched fresh highs this week.</p><h2>Underperformers</h2><p>Ola Electric emerged as the biggest loser after three insolvency petitions were filed against it by vendors. The stock plunged 9.11% to end at ₹40.42. Pine Labs fell 6.86% to close at ₹145.35, while FirstCry was the only new-age tech company to touch an all-time low at ₹202.70.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>I project continued volatility in the Indian tech sector as companies navigate regulatory challenges and market dynamics. The insolvency petitions against Ola Electric signal deeper operational issues that could affect its market position. Swiggy's successful reduction of foreign ownership below 50% threshold positions it favorably under FEMA regulations, though complete IOCC qualification requires additional governance changes.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I anticipate FPI inflows to continue supporting Indian equities unless geopolitical tensions in West Asia escalate further. The banking and IT sectors' strong Q1 performance suggests resilience in traditional sectors, while new-age tech companies face increasing scrutiny on governance and operational practices. Companies with strong domestic ownership structures and clear regulatory compliance pathways will likely outperform in the coming quarters.</p>
