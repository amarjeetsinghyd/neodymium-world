<h2>Manipur's Governance Collapse Exposed Through Delayed Burial</h2><p>The four-month delay in burying BJP MLA Vungzagin Valte reveals Manipur's ethnic governance failure. The Kuki-Zomi community's demands for central investigation into his assault demonstrate their loss of faith in state institutions.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>I analyze this situation as a critical failure of India's internal security apparatus. The state government's inability to protect elected representatives signals to all minorities that they lack political protection. The Zomi Council's demand for separate administrative structure represents a direct challenge to Manipur's territorial integrity.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I project this ethnic conflict will intensify unless New Delhi intervenes decisively. The pattern of targeted violence against minority leaders indicates systematic ethnic cleansing rather than spontaneous mob violence. Without immediate central intervention, Manipur faces permanent fragmentation along ethnic lines.</p><h2>Political Implications for Atmanirbhar Bharat</h2><p>The Manipur crisis directly undermines India's self-reliance ambitions in defense manufacturing. Ethnic instability in border states compromises national security and foreign investment. The central government must recognize that internal security is foundational to economic sovereignty.</p>
