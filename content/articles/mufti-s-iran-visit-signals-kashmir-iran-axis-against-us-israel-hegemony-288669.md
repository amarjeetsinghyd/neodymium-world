<h2>Strategic Implications of Mufti's Tehran Visit</h2><p>I analyze Mufti's attendance at Khamenei's funeral as a calculated geopolitical maneuver that undermines India's carefully balanced foreign policy. Her invitation from Iran's new Supreme Leader Mujtaba Khamenei signals Tehran's strategic outreach to Kashmiri leadership, potentially creating a new axis of resistance against US-Israel dominance in West Asia.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>Mufti's pro-Iran stance during the recent conflict directly contradicted India's official neutrality, revealing fault lines in New Delhi's foreign policy approach. Her status as the only non-Shia political leader from Jammu & Kashmir attending the funeral highlights the unique religious and political positioning she occupies in this diplomatic calculus.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I project that Mufti's Tehran visit will accelerate Iran's strategic penetration of India's internal political landscape, particularly in Kashmir. This alignment could force India to reconsider its defense procurement strategies under Atmanirbhar Bharat, potentially creating supply chain vulnerabilities in critical defense technologies.</p><h2>Regional Power Dynamics</h2><p>The special plane Iran dispatched for Mufti demonstrates Tehran's serious commitment to cultivating relationships with Kashmiri leadership. This diplomatic outreach occurs alongside India's official delegation attending the same funeral, creating a complex diplomatic tableau that reflects the fractured nature of India's foreign policy implementation.</p><h2>Defense Implications</h2><p>Mufti's Iran alignment directly challenges India's strategic autonomy in defense procurement, potentially complicating Atmanirbhar Bharat initiatives. The emerging Kashmir-Iran axis could provide Iran with leverage to influence India's defense decisions, particularly regarding critical technologies and systems where Iran has established expertise.</p>
