<h2>Strategic Analysis: Education Crisis as National Security Threat</h2><p>I assess the NEET paper leak scandal as a critical vulnerability in India's national security framework. The systematic corruption in our premier educational institutions directly undermines the meritocratic foundation essential for our defense establishment and strategic industries.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>The Opposition's demand for Education Minister Pradhan's resignation transcends political theater—it reflects deep public recognition that governance failures in education directly impact national security. When merit becomes negotiable, our defense preparedness faces existential threats.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I project this crisis will accelerate talent flight from India's strategic sectors as qualified professionals seek merit-based opportunities abroad. This brain drain directly undermines our Atmanirbhar Bharat objectives and long-term defense self-reliance.</p><h2>Geopolitical Implications</h2><p>Regional adversaries will exploit these governance failures to advance their strategic narratives about Indian instability. The crisis provides ammunition for external influence operations targeting India's social cohesion.</p>
