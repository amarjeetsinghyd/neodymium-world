<h2>Pakistan's Four-Front Crisis: A Strategic Analysis</h2><p>I analyze Pakistan's deteriorating security situation across four critical fronts. The simultaneous crises in Balochistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan-Occupied Kashmir, and along the Afghan border represent a systemic failure of state authority.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>From my perspective as a defense analyst, Pakistan's security establishment is overstretched. The security-first approach championed by Asim Munir cannot address the political, economic, and social dimensions fueling these uprisings. These crises are interconnected, not isolated incidents.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting</h2><p>I project Pakistan's state authority will continue eroding without addressing root causes. The security vacuum creates opportunities for India to strengthen its strategic position. Regional powers will exploit Pakistan's vulnerability, potentially reshaping South Asian geopolitics.</p><h2>Implications for India</h2><p>India must prepare for potential spillover effects from Pakistan's internal collapse. The Atmanirbhar Bharat initiative in defense procurement gains urgency as Pakistan's instability increases. We should monitor these developments closely for strategic advantage.</p>
