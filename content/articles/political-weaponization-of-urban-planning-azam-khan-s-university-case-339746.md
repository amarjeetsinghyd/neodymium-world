This pattern of targeting opposition-affiliated institutions suggests a broader approach to neutralizing political rivals through regulatory mechanisms.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>I observe that the timing of this demolition order, coinciding with Khan's incarceration, indicates a coordinated strategy to eliminate his remaining influence.
