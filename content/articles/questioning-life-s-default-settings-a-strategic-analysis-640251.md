Default settings in any system, be it an individual or a nation-state, create predictable behaviors and vulnerabilities.</p><h2>Expert Commentary: The Psychology of Strategic Assumption</h2><p>The article's focus on inherited beliefs mirrors how geopolitical doctrines are formed and perpetuated. Nations operate on default settings regarding alliances, economic models, and security postures. The Atmanirbhar Bharat policy is a direct challenge to India's default setting of strategic dependency. I argue that this shift is not merely economic but a fundamental re-calibration of India's default security posture, forcing a re-evaluation of its entire defense industrial base.</p><h2>Strategic Forecasting: The Atmanirbhar Imperative</h2><p>I project that India's deliberate rejection of default dependency settings will accelerate its defense indigenization. This will create new market dynamics and force a realignment of global defense partnerships.
