Their zero-commission model disrupts traditional fee structures across investment banking services.</p><h2>Regulatory Catalyst for Transformation</h2><p>SEBI's tightened derivatives regulations and increased transaction taxes have compressed Zerodha's primary revenue streams. This regulatory pressure accelerates their strategic pivot toward enterprise-grade financial services.</p><h2>Expert Commentary</h2><p>From my analysis, Zerodha's wealth management arm provides unparalleled data advantages for M&A advisory. The domestic capability development strengthens India's financial sovereignty in the global economic order.</p>
